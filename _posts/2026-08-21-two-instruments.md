title: Two instruments

This blog is where the two halves of this site are allowed to talk to each
other. On the [physics page](/physics.html) I keep the record straight —
research, education, projects. On the [photography page](/photography.html)
the pictures speak for themselves. Everything that does not fit either place
lands here.

A camera and an equation are both instruments for paying attention. One
freezes light; the other describes it. I expect most posts will be about what
happens when the two point at the same thing.
